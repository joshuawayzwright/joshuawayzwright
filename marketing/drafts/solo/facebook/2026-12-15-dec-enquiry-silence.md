# Enquiry came in, then silence

An enquiry comes in while you're up a ladder. You mean to reply that night. Then it's three days later and they've booked someone else.

If you work for yourself, the reply is part of the job. A few small habits help:

- Reply within a day, even if it's only "Got your message, I'll send a quote by Thursday."
- Send the quote with what's included and what isn't, so there's less to chase.
- If you hear nothing, follow up once after a few days with a short, friendly message.
- Keep a simple list of open enquiries and quotes, with the date you sent each one.

None of this is clever. It's just being easy to deal with, and it costs nothing.

With the holidays close, it's worth clearing your list before the break so January starts clean.

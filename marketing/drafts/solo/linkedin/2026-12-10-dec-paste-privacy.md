# Three things I wouldn't paste into an AI chat

Three things I wouldn't paste into an AI chat:

1. Client names and contact details. If a job needs a name, use a placeholder and add the real one yourself afterwards.
2. Bank details. Invoices, account numbers and card details stay out.
3. Anything under a confidentiality agreement. If you signed something that says keep it private, don't paste it in.

A simple rule: if you wouldn't be comfortable showing it to a stranger, don't paste it. Check the tool's own privacy settings and terms too.

AI can still help with admin that has none of this in it, like a template for a polite payment reminder.

If you want to look at what's out there, the AI Download Centre is a free directory of AI tools and models, with links to their official download sources.

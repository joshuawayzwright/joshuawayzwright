# Brand rules for all projects

These rules apply to every design, website, document, social post, app, product and marketing asset made for Joshua Wright's projects.

Recorded on 9 November 2026 from the owner's instructions.

## Before creating anything

- Check the existing approved files and examples first. The sources are listed below.
- Ask the owner if an essential asset is missing.
- Don't invent, redraw or replace any logo.
- Show new designs or significant brand changes to the owner for approval before publishing.

## Shared colour palette

| Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Charcoal black | `#0A0A0A` |
| Weathered steel/silver | `#8A8F98` |
| Burnished gold | `#D4AF37` |
| Restrained electric blue | `#0087FF` |

The overall style is polished Australian industrial, with a raw Gold Coast character:

- strong contrast, crisp visuals and readable text;
- professional, accessible layouts;
- consistent across mobile and desktop.

## The Solo Service Provider (commercial business)

- Use the existing approved angular-S identity and business assets.
- Present the full product range fairly. Don't make the AI Marketing Kit appear to be the flagship.
- SOLO AI must use its own approved symbol, not a Gemini-like icon.
- Keep the business mark and the SOLO AI mark distinct.

## WrightWayz (not-for-profit initiative)

- Use the approved human-and-cybernetic handshake inside an industrial gear.
- The phoenix/bird is a secondary motif.
- Brand lines: **"Broken Systems. Unbreakable Bonds."** and **"#WeWalkAsOne"**.
- Keep wording professional and suitable for community, government and funding audiences.
- Don't claim DGR endorsement or charity registration without verification.

## Keep the entities separate

- Use different logos and the correct business name, ABN, contact information, accounts and messaging for each organisation.
- Never mix their finances, customer data, products, or donation and sales flows.

## Before finishing any piece of work

- [ ] Colours match the palette above
- [ ] The correct logo for the correct entity, unaltered
- [ ] Spelling (Australian English), names, ABN and contact details
- [ ] Links work and point to the right entity
- [ ] Mobile and desktop appearance checked
- [ ] Any missing approved asset or instruction reported to the owner

## Known gaps (checked 9 November 2026)

- **Brand fonts:** the brand lock names Montra Bold, Rajdhani Semibold and Inter Regular. No font files are in the repositories, so sites fall back to system fonts.
- **High-resolution masters:** the approved emblems exist only as small crops. The business emblem is 285 × 282 and the SOLO AI emblem is 349 × 306. Don't enlarge them for large-format use; obtain the original masters.
- **WrightWayz phoenix:** there is no separate phoenix/bird asset, only the full emblem and brand board. Don't crop one without approval.

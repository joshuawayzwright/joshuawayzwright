## Summary (checked 16 November 2026; always verify against the canonical file)

- **Website:** https://joshuawayzwright.github.io/ai-download-center/. Free and MIT-licensed.
- **What it is:** a free, searchable directory of AI models, chatbots, assistants, self-hosted apps and creative tools, plus open-source Android APK links.
- **Two kinds of entry:**
  - Hand-picked listings, which link to the official source.
  - Community model cards auto-imported from Hugging Face.
  - Never call the whole catalogue "curated", and never say every link is official.
- **Sponsors:** a featured listing costs $99 USD a month after a 7-day free trial. Check `config.js` for the current price. Every sponsored placement is labelled "Sponsored".
- **Spelling:** the product name is "AI Download Center", with US spelling, in body copy. Use Australian English for everything else. The SOLO site calls it "AI Download Centre". Match the platform's own spelling when you link from SOLO.
- **Banned words:** "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock", "supercharge", "10x".
- **Hard rules:**
  - No invented traffic, users or testimonials.
  - No implied endorsement by any listed company.
  - Disclose paid placement.
  - Follow each platform's self-promotion rules.

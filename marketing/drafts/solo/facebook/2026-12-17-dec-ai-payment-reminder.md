# A polite payment reminder, without the awkwardness

Chasing an overdue invoice is nobody's favourite job. A template can make it quicker and a bit less awkward.

Here's one way to use an AI chat tool for it, safely:

1. Ask it to write a short, polite payment reminder as a template, with blanks like [client name], [invoice number], [amount] and [due date].
2. Don't paste real client names, amounts or bank details into the chat. Fill the blanks in yourself afterwards.
3. Read it before you send it. Check the amount, the due date and the tone are right, and cut anything that doesn't sound like you.
4. Save the version you like and reuse it.

Keep the reminder short: what's owing, since when, how to pay, and a friendly line to get in touch if there's an issue.

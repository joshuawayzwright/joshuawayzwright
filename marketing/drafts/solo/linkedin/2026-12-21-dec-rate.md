# Set your January rate before you restart

If you're quoting for January, check your rate before the first job, not after the first month.

I'm closed from Thursday 24 December and back on Monday 4 January. Before I go, here's the 15-minute check I'd do on any rate for the new year:

1. Costs. Add up a full year of vehicle, tools, phone, insurance and software. Use your bank statements, because memory undercounts.
2. Super. If you want to put money aside, it has to come out of what you charge. Nobody pays it on top for a sole trader. My example used 12%, so check the current rate with the ATO.
3. Unbillable time. Quoting, travel and admin don't appear on an invoice. In my example, only 60% of working hours were billable.
4. Buffer. Slow months and late invoices happen. My example used 15%.

In the worked example I shared earlier this month, those layers took the same $80,000 target (before tax) from about $40 an hour to about $112. Your numbers will differ, so run your own.

General information only, not financial or tax advice. For tax or super questions, ask the ATO or a registered tax agent.

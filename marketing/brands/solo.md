## Voice

- Practical, direct and honest. "No empty promises. Just practical technology, clear progress."
- Australian English (organisation, colour, licence as a noun, practise as a verb).
- Lead with the reader's problem and a concrete next step. Use real numbers only when they come from a source.
- Build in public: share honest progress, including what isn't finished.
- **Who is speaking:** LinkedIn and GitHub posts are written in the first person, as Joshua ("I built…"). The website, email and Google Business Profile posts speak as the business ("we", or "The Solo Service Provider").
- Banned: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock", "supercharge", "10x", "guaranteed results", "passive income", "bestseller".

## Hard rules

- **Australian Consumer Law:** no misleading claims, invented testimonials or fake scarcity. The 30-day money-back guarantee sits alongside the statutory consumer guarantees. Never imply customers waive those rights.
- **Spam Act 2003:** marketing email only goes to people who consented. It must identify The Solo Service Provider and include a working unsubscribe.
- **Keep SOLO separate from WrightWayz.** Never present commercial sales as charity work, and never use WrightWayz to sell SOLO products.

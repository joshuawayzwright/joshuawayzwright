# The 40% of your week nobody pays for

Quick question for the tradies, cleaners, handymen and freelancers here: how much of your working week ends up on an invoice?

Quoting, driving between jobs, admin, invoicing, answering messages and finding the next job all take real time. None of it gets billed.

Here's an example with made-up numbers, so you can swap in your own. Say 60% of your working hours are billable. That means the other 40% still has to be paid for, and the only place it can come from is your hourly rate. Every billable hour carries a share of the hours you can't bill.

In our worked example, with super, business costs and time off already counted, this one item took the rate from about $58 an hour to about $97 an hour. Same income target, very different rate.

Try this over a cuppa this week: guess what share of your hours you really bill, then check it against your calendar and phone for a typical week. Most people find the real number is lower than the guess.

# Which admin job should you hand to AI first?

Which admin job should you hand to AI first? Pick one with no client details in it.

A good starter is a quote follow-up template. It's the message you send when a quote has gone quiet, and it's the same every time apart from a few details.

Try this:
1. Ask the AI for a short, polite follow-up with placeholders, like [client name] and [job].
2. Don't paste in a real client's name, number, address or quote amount.
3. Copy the template into your own email and fill in the details there.
4. Read it before you send it. Change anything that doesn't sound like you.

Before you paste anything into an AI chat, ask: would my client be fine with this being shared? If not, leave it out.

If you're looking for tools to try, the AI Download Centre is a free directory of AI tools and models. Hand-picked listings link to the official source.

# Three places visitors leave a service-business website

Your website might not need more traffic. It might need a better path from "just looking" to "can you do the job?"

Three places a visitor can drop out of a service-business website:

1. The offer is unclear. The homepage describes the business, but not who it helps or what to do next.
2. There's no obvious next step. The call to action is buried, weak, or missing on a phone.
3. Follow-up is slow. An enquiry comes in, then a quote goes out, then silence.

Quick check you can do today: open your site on your phone and ask a friend to find how to book you. If it takes more than a few seconds, that's your first fix.

This is what I built Revenue Rescue around. It's a fixed-scope service for Australian service businesses: a diagnosis of where the customer path breaks, replacement website copy, follow-up message sequences and a 30-day action plan. The work is reviewed by a person, and the scope is agreed before anything on a live site changes.

There's no guarantee of more enquiries or revenue. Results depend on things like traffic, demand, pricing and competition.

## What WrightWayz does today

**Available now** (free, educational):

- **Admin Mindset:** a lived-experience framework for noticing patterns, identifying support, and choosing a manageable next action.
- **WrightWayz Cognition:** a reflection exercise for separating facts from assumptions.
- **Support links:** links to established Australian services, with urgent-help information.

**In development. Never describe these as operating:** structured peer support, scheduled groups, case management, clinical services, AI-assisted functions, and a physical hub.

**Who it's for:** people facing mental-health or addiction-recovery challenges, including isolation, financial hardship, unstable housing, digital exclusion, or rebuilding after incarceration. Also their families, carers and communities. The founding focus is the Gold Coast and South East Queensland.

## Audience and voice

- **Audiences:** the community and people with lived experience; families and carers; local services, government, and funders.
- **Voice:** warm, steady, respectful, and professional enough for government and funding readers. Australian English.
- Use person-first, non-stigmatising language: "person experiencing addiction", not "addict"; "died by suicide", never "committed suicide".
- No hype, no fundraising pressure, no guilt. No sales language of any kind.

## Mental-health safe-messaging rules (mandatory)

Follow Australia's Mindframe guidelines for communicating about mental ill-health, suicide, and alcohol and other drugs.

- Never describe suicide or self-harm methods, locations, or graphic detail. Don't present suicide as a solution or as inevitable.
- Any post that discusses suicide, self-harm, or crisis must include help-seeking information: **Lifeline 13 11 14** and **000 in an emergency**. You may also add other services listed on the WrightWayz support page.
- The resources are educational and don't replace professional care. Say so whenever content could read as advice.
- No before-and-after "recovery success" stories, and no identifiable personal stories without written consent. Mark those `[NEEDS OWNER APPROVAL: consent]`.

# Quotes that go quiet: a simple follow-up routine

Sent a quote to a Gold Coast customer and heard nothing back? It happens to most trades. A short, steady follow-up routine often helps more than another discount.

Here's a simple one:

1. Send the quote with the inclusions and exclusions written down, so there's nothing to guess.
2. After a few days, send one short message: "Hi [name], just checking you got the quote for [job]. Happy to answer any questions."
3. About a week after that, send one more: "Still keen to help if the timing suits. If you've gone another way, no problem."
4. After that, close it off politely and move on.

Keep a simple list of open quotes with the date each was sent. Two minutes on a Friday is enough to see who needs a nudge.

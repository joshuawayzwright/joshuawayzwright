# One job to get done before the break

Before you switch off for the holidays, here's one small job that makes the first week back easier: a January follow-up list.

List every open quote, unpaid invoice and past customer you meant to get back to. Then you can use an AI chat tool to help draft a short, friendly message for each type, such as "checking in on your quote" or "a quick reminder about your invoice".

A quick privacy check first:
- Ask for templates with blanks, not finished messages.
- Keep client names, contact details, amounts and anything under a confidentiality agreement out of the chat.
- Add those details yourself, and read every message before you send it.

Then save the list somewhere you'll find it on the first morning back.

A quick note from us: we're closed from Thursday 24 December and back on Monday 4 January. Thanks for following along this year, and have a safe and relaxing break.

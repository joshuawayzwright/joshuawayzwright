# "About a day" is not a quote

"About a day" sounds like a quote. It's really the start of an argument.

You think it covers the day's labour. The customer thinks it covers materials, the tip run and a second visit if something goes wrong. Nobody is wrong until the invoice lands.

The fix is simple: write down what's in and what's out before the job starts.

For example:
Included: one day's labour, standard materials, clean-up on site.
Not included: extra visits, repairs to existing damage, rubbish removal.
If the scope changes, we agree the new price before the extra work starts.

Three lines like that take two minutes. They save the awkward phone call later, and customers usually appreciate knowing where they stand.

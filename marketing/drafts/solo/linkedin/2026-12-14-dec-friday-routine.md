# A 30-minute Friday money routine

Friday arvo, and the invoices you meant to send are still sitting in your head.

Here's a 30-minute money routine for sole traders. Set a timer and go in this order:

1. Send invoices (10 min). Anything finished this week goes out today, not next week.
2. Chase the overdue ones (10 min). Keep it short and polite: the job, the amount, the due date, how to pay. Start with the oldest.
3. Log the week's costs (10 min). Fuel, materials, tools, software. Photograph receipts now, while you remember what each one was for.

Why this order? Money owed to you comes first. And costs logged weekly are far easier than a shoebox of receipts at tax time.

Finish with one line on paper: what you expect to come in next week.
